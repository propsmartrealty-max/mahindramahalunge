### The Problem With Mature Suburbs: Saturated Price Ceilings
Investors entering Baner, Balewadi, or Aundh today encounter severe structural hurdles:
1. **Inflated Price Baselines:** Mature Baner apartments trade between ₹13,000 and ₹18,000 per sq.ft., severely limiting further 5-year capital appreciation.
2. **Infrastructure Chokepoints:** Narrow arterial feeder roads cannot accommodate expanding vehicular density.
3. **High Density / Low Greens:** Most new standalone towers offer less than 30% open space.

### The Baner Annex Advantage: PMRDA Town Planning Scheme 1
Unlike unplanned urban sprawl, Baner Annex is being developed under **PMRDA Town Planning Scheme 1 (TPS 1)**, modeled after Ahmedabad’s world-class town planning grid. The scheme guarantees:
- 36-meter wide central development plan (DP) boulevards.
- Underground cabling for power, telecom, and water networks.
- Dedicated municipal civic amenity plots for international schools, tertiary hospitals, and central public parks.

### The Transit Breakthrough: 7-Minute Mula River Bypass
The primary growth catalyst for Baner Annex is the newly operational **4-lane Mula River Bridge**. Historically, tech professionals working at Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park in Hinjewadi were forced through the notorious gridlocks at Bhumkar Chowk and Wakad Flyover. 

The Mula River Bridge provides a direct bypass, allowing residents of **[Mahindra Rivenza Baner Annex](https://mahindralifespaceshomes.in/mahindra-rivenza/baner-annex/)** to reach Hinjewadi Phase 1 (Infosys, TCS, Wipro) in just **7 minutes**, while remaining only **8 minutes from Balewadi High Street**.
