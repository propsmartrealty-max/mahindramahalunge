# Inside Pune’s Net-Zero Benchmark: Mahindra Rivenza’s 9-Acre Green Sanctuary & ~44,000 Sq.Ft Clubhouse

*By Sustainable Architecture Review*

In an era of hyper-dense vertical concrete towers, **[Mahindra Rivenza](https://mahindralifespaceshomes.in/)** at Baner Annex (Mahalunge), Pune represents an architectural paradigm shift. Spanning 13.46 contiguous acres, the master layout dedicates **over 70% of its land parcel—more than 9 acres—to vehicle-free biophilic greens and resort amenities**.

### The Biophilic Master Plan
Designed under the principles of biophilic architecture, the master plan reconnects residents with indigenous flora, wind flow channels, and natural sunlight:
1. **Vehicle-Free Ground Surface:** All vehicular traffic is routed into multi-level subgrade parking networks upon entry, keeping the podium and ground levels 100% pedestrian-safe.
2. **Microclimate Moderation:** Over 1,200 native tree species create a natural thermal buffer, lowering ambient summer temperatures by 2 to 3 degrees Celsius compared to surrounding highways.
3. **Net Zero Waste Commitment:** On-site organic waste convertors, 100% treated greywater reuse for landscape irrigation, and pre-certified IGBC Gold green building envelope.

### The ~44,000 Sq.Ft Grand Clubhouse & Resort Amenities
The social centerpiece of the community is a landmark ~44,000 sq.ft. multi-level recreational clubhouse featuring:
- **Resort Swimming Pool with Sunken Aqua Bar:** Complete with shallow lounging ledges, separate heated kid’s splash lagoon, and private cabana decks.
- **Championship Sports Arena:** Floodlit FIFA-dimension futsal arena, indoor wooden-plank badminton courts, and outdoor tennis court.
- **Wellness Sanctuary:** Reflexology walking paths, elevated yoga deck overlooking native flora, and advanced cardio-strength gymnasium.
