# Mahindra Lifespaces Commences Priority Allotments for ₹3,500 Cr Landmark "Mahindra Rivenza" at Baner Annex, West Pune

**PUNE, MAHARASHTRA** — Marking a major urban milestone in Western Maharashtra's golden growth corridor, Mahindra Lifespace Developers Limited has officially commenced priority pre-launch allotments for its flagship 13.46-acre master development, **[Mahindra Rivenza](https://mahindralifespaceshomes.in/)**, situated at Baner Annex (Mahalunge), Pune.

With an estimated Gross Development Value (GDV) of ~₹3,500 Crore, the development sets a new luxury and sustainability benchmark in Pune's booming tech corridor, bridging Baner's lifestyle high-street infrastructure with the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park in Hinjewadi.

### Strategic Location & The 7-Minute Hinjewadi Bypass
Positioned directly along the proposed 36-meter Town Planning Scheme 1 arterial road, **[Mahindra Rivenza Baner Annex](https://mahindralifespaceshomes.in/mahindra-rivenza/baner-annex/)** eliminates the chronic traffic chokepoints of Bhumkar Chowk and Wakad Flyover. Residents benefit from the newly operational 4-lane Mula River Bridge, providing direct 7-minute transit to Hinjewadi Phase 1 tech campuses (housing Infosys, Wipro, and TCS), as well as direct 8-minute connectivity to Balewadi High Street.

### Architectural Scale & Biophilic Sanctuary
- **Land Parcel:** 13.46 Contiguous Acres (Gross Development Value: ~₹3,500 Crore).
- **Open Spaces:** Over 70% vehicle-free landscaped greens (9+ acres of biophilic gardens, reflexology trails, and urban canopy).
- **Grand Clubhouse:** ~44,000 sq.ft. multi-level recreational clubhouse featuring a temperature-regulated swimming pool with a sunken aqua bar, championship squash courts, indoor badminton arena, and coworking executive suites.
- **IGBC Gold Pre-Certification:** Engineered under Mahindra's Net Zero Waste to Landfill philosophy with advanced rainwater harvesting, solar kinetic energy, and organic waste convertors.

### Typologies & Multi-Tier Pricing Architecture
Mahindra Rivenza introduces 9 precision-engineered floor plans across 2, 3, and 4 BHK luxury residences:
- **2 BHK Premium & Luxury:** 635 to 747 sq.ft. RERA carpet area, with launch prices starting from ₹90 Lakhs*.
- **3 BHK Deluxe & Luxury:** 882 to 1,065 sq.ft. RERA carpet area, starting from ₹1.31 Crore*.
- **4 BHK Presidential Sky Villas:** 1,438 to 1,465 sq.ft. RERA carpet area, starting from ₹2.20 Crore*.

Floor plans, detailed CAD layouts, and verified cost sheets are available for inspection at the [Mahindra Rivenza Floor Plans Showcase](https://mahindralifespaceshomes.in/floor-plans/).
